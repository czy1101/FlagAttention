# MSA-001: Enflame BF16 correctness and representative latency

## Environment and scope

- Image: `harbor.baai.ac.cn/flagos-app/vllm0.24.0-enflame-topsl.9.10:2.3.0-0.3.0` in `zwj-enflame-vllm`.
- Assigned container GCU 1 (host GCU 5). The other assigned card was reserved for the GDN2 agent.
- Source: FlagAttention `3912bd75af73ce258267ecac3c508d918f63ee1f`.
- BF16, eager event timing, benchmark warmup 10 ms and measurement 50 ms, three repeats.
- The included benchmark does not support a vLLM reference on Enflame. These are absolute FlagAttention latencies, not speedups.

## Correctness

Selected BF16 prefill/decode cases from `tests/flag_attn/test_minimax_sparse_attention.py`: **4 passed, 15 deselected** in 77.09 s. Exact selection expression and environment setup are in `experiments/msa_eval.py`; raw output is in `experiments/tests.log`.

## Latency

| Stage | Shape `[B,Q,S,KVH,H]` | Total, three runs (ms) | Median total (ms) | Stage times at median run (ms) |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| Prefill | `[1,512,4096,1,8]` | 2.2667, 2.2658, 2.2652 | **2.2658** | score 0.2175, topk 0.2235, attention 1.8971 |
| Decode | `[1,1,16384,1,8]` | 0.2018, 0.1995, 0.2020 | **0.2018** | index decode 0.1615, attention decode 0.1161 |

Stage timings are independent measurements and should not be added to obtain the total. The prefill attention stage accounts for most of the standalone stage time; the decode index stage is a large fraction of standalone decode work. These are profiling leads, not verified optimization opportunities.

## Decision

No kernel change is included. Preserve these correctness and latency numbers as a reproducible starting point for optimization, then profile the attention stage in prefill and index stage in decode on longer shapes.
